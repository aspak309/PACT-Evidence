Privacy Policy

How PACT handles information submitted through the Evidence & Responsibility Record Platform.

Last updated: September 2026

1. About PACT

PACT is an evidence-recording and evidence-packaging platform. It allows users to create records containing information they submit and supporting evidence metadata.

PACT records what the user submits and when the system receives the record. PACT does not independently certify that the underlying event occurred.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use PACT, the platform may process:

  • Your Google account name and email address.
  • Information entered into a PACT record.
  • Reported event date and time.
  • Instruction, organization, agent or system information entered by you.
  • Purpose, authority, limits and outcome information entered by you.
  • Evidence file metadata.
  • Cryptographic fingerprints such as SHA-256 hashes.
  • System-generated PACT record identifiers.
  • Server-recorded creation timestamps.

3. Evidence Files

PACT's initial architecture is designed to minimize the storage of original evidence files.

When evidence is selected during record creation, PACT can calculate a SHA-256 fingerprint locally in the browser. The initial V1 record stores evidence metadata and its fingerprint rather than the original file itself.

Important: A SHA-256 fingerprint is a digital identifier for a file. It is not proof that the contents of the file are truthful or that the underlying event occurred.

4. Google Authentication

PACT uses Google Sign-In for account authentication. Authentication is handled through Firebase Authentication.

PACT uses the authenticated account identifier to associate records with the account that created them.

5. PACT Records

A PACT record may contain information voluntarily entered by the user.

The system also generates a unique PACT ID and records a server-side creation timestamp.

The PACT creation timestamp is intended to represent when the PACT system received the record. It is not a timestamp selected by the user.

6. Reported Dates vs. PACT Creation Time

Users may report when an event allegedly occurred. This reported date is different from the PACT creation timestamp.

A reported event date may describe an event that happened before the PACT record was created.

PACT does not change a user's reported event date into a system-certified event date.

7. How We Use Information

Information processed by PACT may be used to:

  • Create and maintain PACT records.
  • Associate records with authenticated users.
  • Generate PACT IDs.
  • Generate evidence fingerprints.
  • Display record information to the authorized user.
  • Generate evidence receipts.
  • Provide record verification functionality.
  • Maintain platform security and reliability.

8. Data Sharing

PACT does not intend to sell users' personal information.

Information may be processed by infrastructure providers required to operate the service, such as authentication, database, hosting and related technical services.

PACT may also disclose information when required by applicable law or when necessary to protect the security and integrity of the platform.

9. Record Access

PACT records are designed to be associated with the authenticated account that created them.

Access controls are intended to prevent one user from accessing another user's private record information.

Public verification functionality, where provided, should expose only information intended for verification and should not automatically expose private evidence files.

10. Data Security

PACT uses technical controls provided by its infrastructure to help protect account and record information.

No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Users should therefore avoid submitting highly sensitive information unless it is necessary for the intended record.

11. Immutable Records

PACT V1 is designed so that finalized records are not silently edited or deleted through the normal application interface.

If a correction or amendment mechanism is introduced, changes should be represented as a new version or amendment rather than silently rewriting the original record.

12. User Responsibility

Users are responsible for ensuring that the information and evidence they submit is appropriate for their intended use and that they have the necessary rights or permission to submit it.

Users should not submit passwords, private authentication keys, financial credentials, or other secrets into ordinary PACT record fields.

13. No Independent Verification

Important: This record contains information and evidence submitted by the user. PACT records the submission and its server timestamp; it does not independently certify that the underlying event occurred, authorize the action, or assume liability for it.

14. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when PACT's features, infrastructure or legal requirements change.

The latest version published on this page will apply to future use of the service.

15. Contact

If PACT provides a dedicated support or privacy contact channel, privacy-related questions can be submitted through that channel.
