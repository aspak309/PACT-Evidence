How PACT Works

A simple record-first workflow for preserving important information and evidence.

01

Submit Information

Describe what happened, who gave the instruction, what system or person was involved, and what actually happened.

02

Add Evidence

Add supporting evidence such as screenshots, documents, exports or other relevant files. PACT creates a digital fingerprint for submitted evidence.

03

Your record receives a unique PACT ID and a server-recorded creation timestamp that can be referenced later.

What PACT Actually Records

PACT is designed to preserve the record of a submission, not to replace independent investigation, legal authority, or professional verification.
