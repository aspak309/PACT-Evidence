PACT Disclaimer

Important information about what a PACT record does and does not establish.

Last updated: September 2026

Important: A PACT record records information submitted by a user and the time that the PACT system received the record. It does not independently certify that the underlying event occurred.

1. What PACT Is

PACT is an Evidence & Responsibility Record Platform designed to help users create structured records of information and supporting evidence.

A record can include information about an event, instruction, person, organization, AI system, purpose, authority, limits, outcome, and supporting evidence metadata.

2. What PACT Records

PACT records information submitted by the user together with a system-generated PACT ID and a server-recorded creation timestamp.

The PACT creation timestamp indicates when the PACT system received the submission. It does not automatically establish when the underlying event occurred.

3. Reported Event Date

A user may provide a reported event date and time.

This information is user-submitted and may describe an event that occurred before the PACT record was created.

A reported event date should not be confused with the server-generated PACT creation timestamp.

4. Evidence

Users may provide information about supporting evidence. PACT may calculate a SHA-256 fingerprint for selected evidence files.

A cryptographic fingerprint can help identify a particular digital file or data representation.

However: A file fingerprint does not prove that the contents of the file are truthful, authentic, complete, or sufficient to establish that an underlying event occurred.

5. No Independent Verification

Unless a specific future verification mechanism explicitly states otherwise, PACT does not independently investigate, witness, authenticate, or confirm the underlying event described by a user.

The status of a standard PACT record is:

RECORDED

This means that the record was submitted to and recorded by the PACT system. It does not mean that PACT has declared the event to be true.

6. No Government Certification

A PACT record is not automatically a government document, government certificate, regulatory approval, official authorization, or government verification.

7. No Legal Certification

A PACT record is not automatically a legally certified document or a determination of legal fact.

Whether a PACT record is useful or admissible for a particular purpose depends on the circumstances and the requirements of the relevant authority, organization, jurisdiction, or legal process.

8. No Authorization

Creating a PACT record does not authorize an action.

PACT does not grant permission to a person, organization, AI system, software agent, or other system to perform an action described in a record.

9. No Witness Relationship

PACT does not become a witness merely because a user creates a record about an event.

PACT records the user's submission; it does not claim to have personally observed the underlying event.

10. No Guarantee or Insurance

PACT does not guarantee that an event will occur, that an instruction will be followed, that a person or system will act correctly, or that a user will receive a particular outcome.

A PACT record is not an insurance policy and does not itself provide insurance coverage or financial protection.

11. User Responsibility

The person creating a PACT record is responsible for the information they submit.

Users should make reasonable efforts to ensure that their descriptions and evidence information are accurate and that they have the necessary rights or permissions to submit the material.

12. Sensitive Information

Users should avoid placing passwords, private keys, authentication secrets, financial credentials, or other unnecessary sensitive information into ordinary PACT record fields.

13. Platform Availability

PACT is an online technical service. Network failures, maintenance, software errors, infrastructure problems, or other circumstances may temporarily affect availability.

PACT does not guarantee continuous, uninterrupted, or error-free operation.

14. Third-Party Services

PACT may depend on third-party services for authentication, hosting, databases, security, or other infrastructure.

Availability or performance of those services may affect the PACT platform.

15. No Professional Advice

Information displayed by PACT should not be interpreted as legal, financial, medical, regulatory, technical, or other professional advice.

Where professional advice is necessary, users should consult an appropriately qualified professional.

16. Important PACT Statement

This record contains information and evidence submitted by the user.

PACT records the submission and its server timestamp; it does not independently certify that the underlying event occurred, authorize the action, or assume liability for it.
